DISPATCH · 03
Energy · Global Operations

"FI/CO master data. 100+ SAP instances. No central control."

An oil major’s finance data was drifting across more than 100 SAP clients. Each sync attempt produced IDOC errors. SAP Classification — rarely used at this scale — was combined with custom IDOC routing logic to turn a brittle team-intensive process into an automated exception engine.

DISPATCH · 05
Pharma / Agribusiness · Global Platform

"The program director said the initiative would be written off entirely."

A global product-costing platform was on the verge of cancellation. Calculations that should have taken minutes were timing out after hours. The business was preparing to abandon the investment.

The problem wasn’t the algorithm — it was how SAP’s standard modules were being called. Targeted ABAP modifications, data-model restructuring, and parallel processing design resolved what had appeared to be an architectural dead-end. No replatform. No full redesign.

The solution became the global rollout template for subsequent country implementations.

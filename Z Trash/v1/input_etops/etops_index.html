Who we serve

Etops empowers financial institutions across the entire wealth and asset management ecosystem with modular, scalable SaaS solutions. We support asset managers, wealth managers, independent financial advisors (IFAs), family offices, banks, insurance companies, and developers in portfolio management, automating reporting, ensuring regulatory compliance, and delivering superior client experiences.

Pricing

Two basis rates.
No hidden multipliers.

Every engagement is priced from two basis rates — Implementation and Strategic — reduced by a commitment discount as the engagement grows. Billed per sprint for time & materials, monthly for retainers and support. Your exact rate is confirmed in writing within 24 hours, always excl. Swiss VAT (MWST).
